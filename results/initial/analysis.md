## Interpretation and responsible AI

The profiles are synthetic; these are controlled related cases, not a representative sample of NUS students. No personal academic records were used. Fixed offerings, admission assumptions, a limited elective catalogue, and completed internship credit limit realism. This is not a graduation audit or academic advice.
Team members must review source interpretations and labels. Document human and AI contributions in the final report. A solver validates the translated model; only the independent checker evaluates the original request.
Token counts describe subscription usage when supplied; no per-call dollar cost is inferred.
