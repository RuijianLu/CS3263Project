# Project Topic

The term project constitutes an important and interesting part of this module. The project aims to allow you to examine a particular aspect of AI in more depth. We encourage you to choose a project topic from one of the following 2 themes. But if you want to work on a different topic, it should be related to one or more of the AI topics covered in the module.

- AI assistant for X (where X is a domain such as education, finance, well being, elderly care, customer service, etc.)
- AI for good, in areas such as: health, education, sustainability, climate change

Look for an AI sub-problem (at least a simplified idealized version of the sub-problem) in one of the themes that can be formulated as one of the problems discussed in the course: constraint satisfaction, logical reasoning, planning, probabilistic reasoning, sequential decision making. You may also wish to look at problems from competitions, e.g. <https://mlcontests.com/>. Described how the sub-problem can be formulated to a sufficient extent that a tool can be used to solve the problem. Construct problem instances of the sub-problem, described in natural language or other natural modalities (or download them if available). The larger the number of problem instances, the more convincing any evaluation you do will be, but this will be limited by the resources that you have available. Use a frontier AI model to solve the problems described in natural language directly and compare it to using the model to translate the problem description into a specification that can be solved using a tool for solving the problem. Use evidence from your evaluation, including whether the problem instances can be solved using the different approaches, to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of each approach. It may be the case that the problems are better solved using tools not covered in this course, e.g. linear programming or by training a non-probabilistic classifier, or that translating the problem into a form that can be solved using a tool is not a good approach (e.g. knowledge required by the tool is not easily obtained). These issues and variations of practical situations, e.g. availability of solver, data, and types of inputs, should be discussed as well and if time permit, alternative approaches can be examined in the project. Recognizing the limitations of the tools is an important aspect of the project.

  - Methodology
  - Formulation of problem/subproblems into mathematical/computational problems related to formulations covered in the course. If applicable, alternative approaches described at a similar level of detail as covered in the course.
  - _Literature survey and prior work_ (_if applicable)_
  - _Implementation_
  - Analysis and insights. This is where your project can stand out beyond an average project. Pick a few relevant parts to do deeper analysis. Questions you can analyse include (but is not limited to) the difficulty of various parts of the problem (e.g. state space size, size of representation of different components such as transition function of MDP, worst-case time for solving the problem, etc.), importance of different parts of your solution (e.g. you may do ablation studies in your experiments removing parts of your solution and measuring the decrease in performance, etc.), whether solving the problem directly by LLM or translating it for tool use is better and justifying your solution choice (through analysis of properties of the problem and of the method, etc.) as well as your evaluation choice (through analysis of problem properties and needs), etc. You should also examine carefully **assumptions** that are used in the construction of the problem instances. Are the assumptions and problem instances realistic? Are they representative of the problems that are likely seen in practice, or is constructed to examine specific issues? What are cases that can cause failures in the assumptions (are they common, is the agent likely to be deployed in an adversarial environment, etc.) and are there ways to handle them?

# Appendix: Some Example Project Ideas

\[Ref: ChatGPT 4.0\]

1\. Elderly Companion Chatbot:

- Description: Consider an AI chatbot that helps combat loneliness in the elderly. The chatbot should have features to remind users about medication, tell stories, play games, etc. Study a subproblem within this domain.

2\. Personalized Learning Assistant:

- Description: Consider a chatbot that aids in the learning process. Depending on the user's performance, the assistant recommends resources, quizzes, or breaks. Study a subproblem within this domain.

3\. AI Tour Guide:

- Description: Consider an AI agent that provides virtual tours of famous landmarks or museums, tailoring its information to users' interests and prior knowledge. Study a subproblem within this domain.

4\. Eco-Friendly Lifestyle Assistant:

- Description: Consider an AI that provides tips, resources, and reminders to help users lead a more eco-friendly lifestyle, from reducing energy consumption to waste management. Study a subproblem within this domain.

5\. AI Reading Companion:

- Description: Consider a chatbot that helps enhance the reading experience. As users read a book, the AI can provide summaries, context, or answer questions about the content. Study a subproblem within this domain.

6\. Interactive Cooking Assistant:

- Description: Consider a voice-activated assistant to help users while cooking. It can read recipes aloud, answer questions about ingredients, and suggest substitutions. Study a subproblem within this domain.
